About the founder

Dr. Sheetal R. Ganput

Founder & Managing Director, Stratify N.V.

A strategic advisor, governance professional, and organizational development specialist with great experience leading organizations through complex transformation and continuous improvement initiatives.

As the Founder & Managing Director of Stratify N.V., she partners with organizations to strengthen governance, risk management, compliance, information security, and leadership. She has extensive experience in the design, implementation, certification, and continual improvement of international management systems, including ISO 27001, ISO 27701, ISO 42001, ISO 22301, ISO 9001, and ISO 31000. In addition, she has successfully led enterprise-wide initiatives in cyber resilience, business continuity, and supplier development, including the implementation of the Bluewave Supplier Development Program.

Sheetal is recognized for her ability to translate complex challenges into practical, sustainable solutions that deliver measurable business value. Her approach combines strategic thinking with a people-centered mindset, driven by the belief that lasting organizational success is achieved when governance, processes, technology, and people work in harmony.

Passionate about leadership and organizational excellence, she inspires executives, teams, and professionals to navigate change with confidence, build resilient organizations, and create sustainable impact.
